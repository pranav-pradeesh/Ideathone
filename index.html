    How it works

    An hour is a decision drill, not a hackathon.

    You are judged on choosing one idea over three others and defending it — not on what you can build in sixty minutes.

    Three people, maximum

    Four cannot agree inside a five-minute decision block.

    One role each

    Agreed before you arrive, so nobody spends the first ten minutes deciding who does what.

    Five slides, cutoff at 48:00

    Problem · Idea · How it works · Impact · Ask. Late decks are not judged.

    Phone only

    Everything runs on your phone. Come with a full battery — sockets are not guaranteed.

    Any AI app you like

    For research and for building the slides. Closed when you choose the idea, and when you pitch.

    Nothing prepared in advance

    The challenges are handed out at the venue. A pre-built deck removes a team.

    AI is a tool here, not a contestant
