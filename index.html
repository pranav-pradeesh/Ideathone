    How it works

    Ninety minutes is a decision drill, not a hackathon.

    You are judged on choosing one idea over three others and defending it — not on what you can build in ninety minutes.

    Three or four people

    Three is the minimum, four the maximum. The fourth member is optional.

    One role each, five to choose from

    Every team needs a Team Lead, a Presentation Maker and a Researcher. Named at registration.

    Five slides, cutoff at 3:36 pm

    Problem · Idea · How it works · Impact · Ask. Late decks are not judged.

    Laptop or phone

    Whichever you work faster on. A phone is enough for all of it. Come charged — sockets are not guaranteed.

    Any AI app you like

    For research and for building the slides. Closed when you choose the idea, and when you pitch.

    Nothing prepared in advance

    The challenges are handed out at the venue. A pre-built deck removes a team.

    Splitting the ninety minutes

    Seven blocks between 2:30 and 4:00. Orange means AI is allowed; red means it is closed.

    Organisers: the pitch block is the constraint

    The 24-minute pitch block fits 8 teams. Beyond that, run parallel pods with a judge each.

    AI is a tool here, not a contestant

    Use it for research and slides. The two blocks that decide the winner — choosing the idea, and the pitch — are AI-free.
