Role book

Who does what, and when

A team of three has about 180 person-minutes in this event, and loses most of them to overlap if nobody has decided who owns what. Each member takes one of these three roles, agreed before you arrive. No two members of a team hold the same role.

Register your team

How to use this

Read your own role before you arrive; skim the other two. On the day, the only question that should ever be asked out loud is "what minute are we on?" — the answer to "who is doing this?" is already written down here.

Every minute reference below maps to the seven-block schedule. Times are minutes since your team's timer started, not clock time.

The three rules that survive everything

  • The Lead owns the clock. If a phase overruns, that is the Lead's miss, not the team's bad luck.
  • One file, one owner. After minute 30 only the Storyteller touches the deck. Two people editing one deck at minute 42 is how teams miss the cutoff.
  • Nothing unverified reaches a slide. Whoever found a fact is the person accountable for it in Q&A.
