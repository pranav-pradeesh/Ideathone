Read this before you rely on it

  • This list lives in this browser on this device. Clearing site data wipes it. Download the master sheet after every import.
  • A team registering on their own phone is stored on their phone — you only get it when they send you the file. For a single registration desk, register everyone on one laptop and this page is the whole system.
  • A re-saved .xlsx (opened and saved again in Excel) cannot be imported. Export it to CSV and import that.
