🏗️ Bridge Building Challenge

Fifty sticks. Forty minutes. One load test.

Design and build a bridge from the materials provided, then watch it take load. Twelve sections, numbered — entering means you accept them. This is a separate event from the Ideathon, with its own teams and its own registration; entering one does not enter you in the other.

If you remember only three things

  • Fifty sticks, not fifty-one. Going over is a disqualification, not a deduction.
  • Hands off at 2:15 pm. Construction after that disqualifies the team.
  • It has to survive the test. Twenty of the fifty marks are load-bearing; a beautiful bridge that folds scores nothing there.
